# Rules — Gemini 4 Argon SF Hackathon

Sources: official details page https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/gemini-4-argon-sf/details (event record + waiver, fetched 25.10 12:15 PDT) and the Google builder guide (BUILDER_GUIDE.md). The waiver text below is binding; the summary is ours.

Official participant guide: PARTICIPANT_GUIDE.md (overrides anything below that conflicts).

## Deadline

- **Submissions due 5:00 PM PT today** (guide schedule; matches 00:00 UTC in the event record). Platform shows a 1-hour grace, but judging starts at 5:00, so treat 5:00 as hard.
- Submission needs: public repo, **1-minute demo video**, accessible demo link, all team members added.

## Hard rules

- Build in Problem Statement One (Million Token Engineering) or Two (More Dimensions).
- Repo public. Team 1–4. New work only; demo must show only what was built today, or immediate disqualification.
- No code/data/assets without rights. Banned list in PARTICIPANT_GUIDE.md (no RAG, Streamlit, dashboards-as-product, medical advice, image analyzers, etc.).
- In person, Shack15, Ferry Building Ste 201.

## Judging

Round 1 (rooms, ~3 min live demo + Q&A): working live demo with real verification loop **50%**, problem/usefulness 25%, originality (only possible with Argon) 15%, pitch clarity without slides 10%. Round 2: top 6 on stage, same criteria, equal weights.

Judges: Google DeepMind team; final round Joana Carrasqueira, Shudhatma Jain, Subhasmita Sahoo, Oskar Yao. Prizes: 1st $7,500 · 2nd $5,000 · 3rd $2,500 · Best use of Managed Agents $5,000.

## Waiver highlights

- IP: you keep ownership, but grant Cerebral Valley + partners a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use/modify/publish the submission. **Don't submit Helios proprietary code or customer data.**
- Nothing you share is confidential; photo/video/audio recording consent.
- Personal info may be shared with event partners.
- Under-18s need a guardian present. Professional conduct; organizers may remove people.
- Seating/power/equipment may be limited. California law, SF courts.
