# Gemini 4 Argon SF Hackathon

- **When:** Sun, Oct 25 at 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM (PDT)
- **Where:** San Francisco, CA
- **Hosts:** [Cerebral Valley](https://cerebralvalley.ai/u/cv), [Google DeepMind](https://cerebralvalley.ai/u/deepmind)
- **Format:** Hackathon (submissions due Oct 26, 2026 UTC)
- **Registration:** Join Waitlist – https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/gemini-4-argon-sf
- **Page:** https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/gemini-4-argon-sf

### **Gemini 4 Argon is here. What will you build with it?**

Push the boundaries of what's possible with Argon. Join Google DeepMind in San Francisco to explore the frontier, from advanced reasoning and visual coding to robotics and world models.

We're looking for ambitious projects that push Argon to its limits. We want to see something that wasn't possible last week.

### **Prizes**

We've got $20K in cash prizes lined up for winning teams. 

- **First place:** $7,500
- **Second place:** $5,000
- **Third place:** $2,500
- **Best use of Managed Agents:** $5,000

We’ll have the Google DeepMind team in-house for judging and speaking, including [Ivan Leo](https://x.com/ivanleomk) as a speaker, alongside our final-round judges Joana Carrasqueira, Shudhatma Jain, Subhasmita Sahoo, and Oskar Yao from Google.

***Note:**** This event is fully in-person in San Francisco. Space is limited. Maximum team size is four. All attendees must apply and be approved. Submitting an application does not guarantee attendance.*
