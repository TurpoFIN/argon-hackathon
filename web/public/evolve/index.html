Why this is AlphaEvolve-style

  • Programs, not parameters. Every candidate is a full Python apply(city) plan. Flash edits it with SEARCH/REPLACE diffs; Argon directs every generation and writes a crossover of two elites.
  • A machine-checkable evaluator. The storm, solvers, damage curves and cited cost table are locked and deterministic: same plan, same ledger, so the score can't be talked up.
  • An evolutionary database. A MAP-Elites archive keeps the best plan for each strategy and cost band, so the search stays diverse instead of collapsing on one idea.
