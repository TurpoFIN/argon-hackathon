# Preventive work versus doing nothing

The main planning surface compares physical work packages with a no-work baseline. Calls and inspections are not counted as mitigation. Argon chooses only supplied work IDs and qualified crew IDs; Python checks time, crew concurrency, stock and equipment before a result is saved.

The work catalog covers inlet clearing, securing loose items, closing accessible windows, relocating contents, fitting an existing barrier, sandbag construction, deploying a compatible pump on backup power, arborist work, engineered support, light route clearance and authorized traffic control. Locations come from the existing public inventory and storm reports. A report is a reason to consider a location, not evidence that a defect or vulnerable item is present now.

## Resource and time meaning

Three explicit resource scenarios are available. Basic uses the existing general-crew count, with two people per crew, hand tools/PPE, twenty tie-downs and one cart. Staged adds an already compatible barrier, sandbag material, pump/backup connection and two-person electrical team. Specialist adds qualified arborist, building and traffic teams and their equipment. These are transparent planning scenarios, not claims about municipal inventories or bookings. Operator-entered zero specialist capacity overrides a package.

The existing scenario's crew staging coordinates anchor estimated walking travel. Bounds use straight-line distance with a 1.3–1.8 street detour factor at 80–60 metres/minute. They are not surveyed routes. Travel, setup and work all consume the preparation window. The upper bound is used for feasibility and the schedule; tools cannot be double-booked, and placed barriers, pumps, tie-downs and materials stay committed. A partly completed package receives no completed-work credit.

Time ranges are openly labeled planning estimates. The inlet work range is anchored to a municipal fifteen-minute maintenance-visit budget, not a timed SF study. FEMA P-234 supplies the approximately one-hour, two-person reference for filling and placing one hundred sandbags. Other ranges describe bounded work scopes, not measured production rates. Purchasing, fabrication, permits and engineering are excluded from simple deployment tasks and remain prerequisites.

## Benefit evidence

Each comparison separates:

- What physically changes if the package is completed, versus doing nothing.
- The source's actual observation or operating guidance.
- Conditions where the package gives no benefit or may fail.
- What cannot be inferred locally.

The Sandy example is a real operational observation: four more basement pumps were connected to emergency power just before the storm; six pumps controlled water below three inches and generators stayed operational. This does not isolate a causal effect or validate any pump's performance at an SF site. The tree evidence is an external observational association, not a causal/local pruning percentage. Local glass investigations support failure mechanisms, not a prevention rate. Inlet-clearing accounts and relocation guidance support sensible work but do not establish a matched local loss reduction.

The no-work baseline uses zero crew time and equipment. Its damage is not invented. Neither an action nor the baseline receives a numerical loss estimate, avoided-volume total or aggregate risk score. Unknown current defects, entry paths, loads and occupancy remain unknown. An already clear inlet, already latched window or absent vulnerable item gives no incremental benefit.

## Boundaries

A conditional scheduled work package is not a dispatch, completed work, site approval or measured outcome. Structural support and traffic closures remain unavailable without the relevant site design/authority; adding equipment never creates those approvals. Public powerline maps never imply a building connection or permission for general crews to do utility work.

The TimesFM and hydrology research code remains owned by the other chat. This feature does not recalibrate that model or use its synthetic damage totals as storm validation. Saved comparisons are reused only when their source, weather, site, resource and time fingerprint remains current.
